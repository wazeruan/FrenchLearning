# Roadmap: from zero to NCLC 7 (TEF Canada / TCF Canada)

**Goal:** NCLC 7 in all four skills (listening, reading, writing, speaking) on TEF Canada or TCF Canada,
for the French-language Express Entry category and up to 50 CRS points.
**Pace:** ~30 minutes per day. Realistic horizon from zero: about two years of steady practice.
**Test choice:** undecided. The app trains the shared skills and both formats; decide nearer the test date.

| NCLC | ≈ CEFR | TCF Canada (CO / CE / EE / EO) | TEF Canada (CO / CE / EE / EO) |
|------|--------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| 7    | B2-    | 458 / 453 / 10 / 10            | 249 / 207 / 310 / 310          |

Score thresholds come from third-party summaries of IRCC's equivalency charts. Before registering,
check the official IRCC tables on canada.ca, since TEF scoring scales have changed.

## Stages

| Stage | Level | Focus | Status |
|-------|-------|-------|--------|
| 1 | A1 (≈ NCLC 1–3) | Core vocabulary, être/avoir, articles and gender, present tense of -er verbs, negation, questions, numbers to 100, time, first dialogues in Canadian settings | **in progress** |
| 2 | A2 (≈ NCLC 4) | passé composé, futur proche, reflexive verbs, pronouns; housing, work, health, transport, banking, government services | planned |
| 3 | Exam trainer | Timed CO/CE sets in TCF and TEF formats; EE tasks with timer, word count, model answers and checklists; EO tasks with recording and transcript; an NCLC estimate per skill; optional AI examiner (Claude API, sent only on request) | planned |
| 4 | B1 (≈ NCLC 5–6) | imparfait, conditionnel, relative pronouns, connectors; giving opinions; formal letters and emails | planned |
| 5 | B2 (≈ NCLC 7) | subjonctif, argumentation, news and society topics, full mock exams | planned |

## A daily 30-minute session

1. **Review** (~5 min): spaced-repetition words that are due.
2. **Next step** (~15 min): the next lesson on the path (words, then grammar or dialogue, then practice).
3. **Skill drill** (~10 min): listening (dialogue), speaking (say it aloud), and later the exam tasks.
